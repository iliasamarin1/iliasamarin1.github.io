01 / User research
Role
User researcher, team of 6
When
Fall 2026
Methods
Heuristic evaluation, 12 think-aloud tests, affinity mapping

Do students notice when ChatGPT is wrong? Mostly, no.

0 / 12noticed what the summary left out
4 / 12checked the source
33%found the regenerate button
  • Long, well-formatted answers hide omissions. People skim the bold text and trust the structure.
  • When ChatGPT accepted a wrong correction, two participants accepted it too.
  • 9 of 12 valued speed, so any fix has to add almost no friction.

Where we landedHow might we help students catch what ChatGPT leaves out or changes, without slowing them down?

SneakySwing: our retention hypothesis was wrong

An AI golf coach with a live MVP. We ran 11 interviews, 7 of them on the course, to test usability, trust and retention.

HypothesisResultWhat we heard
The app is easy to usePartly"Even I'm unsure what some of these things mean." Four-year golfer
Strong golfers trust AI lessConfirmed"Someone taller is going to have a steeper swing. Will it know that?"
Strong golfers stay for the edgeDisproven"You can essentially coach yourself."

Strong golfers had zero willingness to pay, so we pointed the founders toward beginners. We also delivered six fixes, from reliable video capture to calibrating for each golfer's height and style.

Live
oryn-shop.com

Oryn: an iron supplement built from 3,600 customer voices

Before building, I read Reddit, YouTube, TikTok and Amazon reviews from women who are tired in a way sleep doesn't fix.

What I found

  • Her real competitor is quitting. "Take iron for a week, feel awful, quit."
  • She blames herself. "I told myself I was lazy."

What I built

  • A gentle once-a-day formula, so she stays on it long enough to work.
  • Copy in her words: "Feel like yourself again."
  • Multi-month plans, because iron takes months to work.

Oryn, a brand I launched

Built from 3,600
customer voices.

Before building anything, I read Reddit, YouTube, TikTok and Amazon reviews from women who are tired in a way sleep doesn't fix. One line kept coming back:

Take iron for a week, feel awful, quit.

What I learned

  • Her real competitor is quitting, not another brand.
  • She blames herself: "I told myself I was lazy."
  • She no longer trusts the word "gentle."

What I built

  • A gentle once-a-day formula, so she stays on it long enough to work.
  • Copy in her words: "Feel like yourself again."
  • Multi-month plans, because iron takes months.
  • When no supplier could make the first version, I cut scope and launched anyway.
